Match instructions

Rules you set before the match, which your side carries out when their conditions are met. Each one waits for a minute, a score or an event, then changes the shape, the team instructions or the players on the pitch. A rule fires once, and they are checked in the order listed.

{{ substitutionsNote() }}

On, your assistant swaps tired players at the usual windows after your instructions have had their say. Off, only your own substitutions are made, and injuries are still covered.
