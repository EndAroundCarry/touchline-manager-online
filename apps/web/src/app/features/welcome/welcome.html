Touchline Manager

An old-school football management game with a modern online structure. You take over a club, prepare it asynchronously, and compete against other managers on fixed matchdays.

Matchdays
Tuesday, Thursday, Sunday
19:00 UTC. Team sheets lock 30 minutes before.
Season
34 matchdays
Home and away against every club in your division.
Squad
18 to 25 players
At least two goalkeepers must be registered.
