Programmes

Each player trains one programme. Unless you choose otherwise it is the one that matches their position (TRN-1, TRN-2). A programme spreads the same daily development over the attributes it trains, so a narrower programme grows each of them faster; the younger the player, the more there is to spread. Older players slowly lose pace and stamina, and a programme that trains an attribute slows its decline.
